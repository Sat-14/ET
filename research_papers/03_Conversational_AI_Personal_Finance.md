# Conversational AI in Personal Financial Management - Research Summary

## Primary Paper: Revolutionizing Finance with Conversational AI

### Paper Link
- **Nature:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41599-025-04725-y
- **Journal:** Humanities and Social Sciences Communications, Volume 12, Article 388 (2025)

### Focus Areas
- ChatGPT application and management strategies in financial services
- Financial customer service, financial planning, risk management
- Portfolio analysis, insurance services, fraud prevention

### Key Findings
- 76% of banking institutions are implementing or planning to implement AI-powered customer service
- Conversational AI is stepping beyond traditional chatbots to predict needs, personalize financial advice, and automate complex processes
- Chat interfaces enhance literacy and engagement versus traditional app UIs
