## Proposal Overview
ICAI (Institute of Chartered Accountants of India) has proposed introducing an **optional joint taxation system** for married couples in India, first presented in pre-Budget 2025 suggestions and reiterated for Budget 2026-27.

## Key Features
- **Optional scheme** - couples can choose individual or joint filing
- **Basic exemption limit**: Rs 6 lakh for joint filing (double the individual limit)
- **Proposed joint tax slabs:**
  - No tax: up to Rs 6 lakh
  - 5%: Rs 6-14 lakh
  - 10%: Rs 14-20 lakh
  - 15%: Rs 20-24 lakh
  - (Higher slabs continue)

## Objectives
1. Provide relief to families with a single earning member
2. Reduce tax evasion and income-splitting schemes
3. Recognize the household as a single economic unit
4. Discourage artificial income transfers between spouses

## Current Status (as of 2025)
- CBDT committee is reviewing ICAI's recommendations
- Tax experts believe full implementation is unlikely in near term
- Discussions are ongoing
